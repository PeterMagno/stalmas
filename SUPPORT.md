# Support

[Español](SUPPORT.es.md)

STALMAS has no user accounts and no central servers. Here is where to get help depending on what you need:

- **Questions & troubleshooting with the community:** ask in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/PeterMagno/stalmas/discussions/categories/q-a). Never paste passwords, API tokens, private keys, real IPs or domains.
- **Guides & documentation:**
  - [Demo guide](docs/demo.md) · [FAQ](docs/faq.md) · [Free vs Pro](docs/free-vs-pro.md) · [Privacy](docs/privacy.md)
  - Full manuals on the website: [stalmas.app/en/docs/](https://stalmas.app/en/docs/)
- **Report a bug:** open an issue using the [Bug report template](https://github.com/PeterMagno/stalmas/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml), or from the app go to **Settings → About & legal → Report a problem** to prepare a reviewable email draft (with sensitive data automatically hidden) to `report@stalmas.app`.
- **Private matters, billing, privacy requests or security:** email **support@stalmas.app** (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities).

You can write in English or Spanish.
