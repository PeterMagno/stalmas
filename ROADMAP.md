# Roadmap

[Español](ROADMAP.es.md)

STALMAS follows a phased release plan without fixed dates or unshipped feature promises.

## 1. Closed testing on Google Play *(current)*
- 40-spot closed test to validate stability, device compatibility across Android 12+ hardware, and day-to-day workflows.
- Iterative fixes and updates shipped to the closed testing track based on tester reports.

## 2. Production launch
- Public release of STALMAS v1.0 on Google Play.
- Delivery of lifetime STALMAS Pro promo codes to testers who completed the 14-day test and shared at least 2 bugs or ideas.

## 3. After launch
- **Ideas are prioritized based on what you vote for in [Discussions → Ideas](https://github.com/PeterMagno/stalmas/discussions/categories/ideas).**
- Open a topic in **Ideas** or vote with 👍 on existing proposals to help shape what comes next.
