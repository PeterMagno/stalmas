# Tester program (Closed Testing)

[Español](testers.es.md) · [Apply on the website](https://stalmas.app/en/testers/)

Before launching publicly on Google Play, STALMAS needs a closed test of **at least 12 active testers for 14 consecutive days**. We are recruiting with margin: **40 spots maximum**.

## What is offered
- **STALMAS Pro free** during the entire closed test.
- Testers who **complete the 14 days** and **share at least 2 bugs or ideas** keep **STALMAS Pro for life**.

## What is asked
- A phone running **Android 12 or later** and a **Google account** (used on Google Play).
- Install STALMAS from the closed-test opt-in link and use it for **at least 14 days**.
- Share **at least 2 bugs or ideas** (on [GitHub](https://github.com/PeterMagno/stalmas/issues/new/choose) / [Discussions](https://github.com/PeterMagno/stalmas/discussions), or from **Settings → About & legal → Reports** inside the app).

## Dates & sign-up
- **Sign-up page:** [stalmas.app/en/testers/](https://stalmas.app/en/testers/)
- Exact sign-up and test dates are published on that page once the test window opens. Sign-up closes on the announced end date or when all 40 spots are filled.

## Minimum data & privacy
- The sign-up form only asks for your **Google email (Gmail)** and an **alias** (plus the commitment and consent checkboxes).
- The form does **not** store data: it forwards your request to `testers@stalmas.app`, and selected emails are added to the closed-test list in Google Play Console solely to invite you, send test updates and deliver your Pro code.
- **All tester emails and the tester list are deleted at the latest 30 days after the test ends** (or earlier if you email `support@stalmas.app`). Full details: [stalmas.app/en/privacy/#testers](https://stalmas.app/en/privacy/#testers).

## What to do during the 14 days
- Expect **2–3 updates** on the closed track during the test, with release notes published in [Releases](https://github.com/PeterMagno/stalmas/releases) and `docs/releases/`.
- Keep the app installed and try real tasks or the demo server. We will also send a reminder halfway through with a quick check.

### Suggested Week 1 task
Try the app **first without entering your own server credentials**:
1. Follow the [Demo guide](demo.md) to add `demo.stalmas.app` (username `demo`, password `stalmas-demo`).
2. Open the **`homelab`** environment.
3. Inspect the failing **`worker`** container and **ask the AI assistant why `worker` fails**.
4. Report any UI glitch, unclear text, crash or improvement idea you spot along the way.

## How the lifetime Pro code is delivered
Once the 14-day test finishes and STALMAS launches in production on Google Play, testers who completed the 14 days and contributed at least 2 bugs or ideas will receive their **Google Play promo code for lifetime STALMAS Pro** at the email address they registered with.
