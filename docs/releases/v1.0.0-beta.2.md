# STALMAS v1.0.0-beta.2 — Closed testing

[Español](v1.0.0-beta.2.es.md)

Closed-testing release of **STALMAS** on Google Play (versionCode 10). **Want to join?** [Apply as a tester](https://stalmas.app/en/testers/) (40 spots, Pro free during the test).

### Highlights
- **Direct connections, no middleman:** manage Portainer CE/BE (API Key or Username/Password, optional self-signed TLS) and Linux SSH hosts (password or `.pem`/`.key` with TOFU host key verification) straight from your Android 12+ phone.
- **Dashboard, containers, logs, stacks & resources:** health overview, start/stop/restart/pause/rename/update containers, live CPU/RAM/processes, colorized ANSI logs with error/warning filters and `.log` export, Compose/Git/Template stacks, images, volumes, read-only networks and safe cleanup.
- **SSH Terminal (`xterm-256color`):** Esc, Tab, Ctrl and arrow keys, command history and snippets.
- **Hybrid AI Assistant:** floating assistant with cloud Gemini (your own API key, BYOK) and Privacy Gatekeeper (hides IPs, domains, passwords and keys), dangerous command confirmation, plus Pro features: on-device local AI (Gemma 4 models), reversible IP/host pseudonyms, one-tap diagnostics, custom dashboard, usage history, background alerts, single-container widgets and encrypted AES-256 Google Drive backup.
- **Security:** local SQLCipher (AES-256) database and AES-256-GCM preferences protected by the Android Keystore, PIN/biometric lock, zero ads and zero analytics.

### What to test
1. **Demo server first:** follow the [Demo guide](../demo.md) (`demo.stalmas.app`, user `demo`, password `stalmas-demo`), open `homelab`, inspect the intentionally failing `worker` container and ask the AI assistant why it fails.
2. **Your own Portainer & SSH servers (optional):** test self-signed TLS, container actions, log filtering, stack editing/deployment and SSH TOFU fingerprint verification.
3. **AI Assistant:** test cloud Gemini with your own key (check that private data is redacted) and, if your phone has 64-bit CPU + 6–8 GB RAM, test local Gemma 4 models in Pro.
4. **Security & Pro tools:** test PIN/biometrics, themes, home-screen widgets, background alerts and encrypted Drive backup.

> **Found a bug or have an idea?** [Open an issue](https://github.com/PeterMagno/stalmas/issues/new/choose) (never paste passwords, tokens, IPs or domains!) or use **Settings → About & legal → Reports** inside the app.
